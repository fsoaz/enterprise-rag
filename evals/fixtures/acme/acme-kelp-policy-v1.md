# Kelp — Marine sensor storage

This fictional procedure applies only to Acme's Kelp operation. It does not govern other operations or organizations.

## Revision 1

Marine sensor storage humidity is checked every 10 days.

The stores keeper replaces a saturated desiccant pouch.

## Exceptions
