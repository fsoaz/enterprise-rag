# Yarrow — Robotic cell handover

This fictional procedure applies only to Acme's Yarrow operation. It does not govern other operations or organizations.

## Revision 1

A robotic cell handover includes 4 dry cycles.

The receiving operator signs the gripper alignment record.

## Exceptions

Exceptions to this procedure use routing code ROUTE-YARROW. Consult the separate register to identify the destination.
